CivicLens

Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 27, 2026  ·  North Carolina, United States

Plain-English summary CivicLens does not store, sell, or share your address or any personal information. Your address is used only to look up your representatives in real time and is discarded when your session ends. No account is required. No cookies track you.

1. Who we are

CivicLens is a nonpartisan civic education tool built for North Carolina voters to explore their elected representatives, track legislation, and compare congressional districts. It is an independent student project and is not affiliated with any government agency, political party, or campaign.

2. Information you provide

The only information you enter into CivicLens is a street address used to identify your district and representatives. Here is exactly what happens to it:

  • Your address is sent to OpenStreetMap Nominatim to convert it into geographic coordinates (latitude and longitude).
  • Those coordinates are sent to the OpenStates API and U.S. Census TIGERweb to retrieve your representatives and district boundaries.
  • ✓ Not stored Your address is never written to a database, file, or log by CivicLens.
  • ✓ Not sold We do not sell, rent, or share your address with any third party.
  • ✓ Session only A short-lived memory cache (max 24 hours) avoids repeat API lookups for the same address. This cache exists only in the running server's memory and is cleared on every restart.

3. Information collected automatically

CivicLens is hosted on Streamlit Community Cloud. Streamlit may collect standard server logs (IP address, browser type, pages visited) for operational purposes in accordance with Streamlit's own privacy policy. CivicLens does not access or control these logs.

CivicLens does not use cookies, tracking pixels, analytics scripts, or advertising of any kind.

4. Third-party services

CivicLens calls the following external APIs to deliver its features. Each service receives only the minimum data necessary:

  • OpenStreetMap Nominatim — receives your address to return coordinates.
  • OpenStates API — receives coordinates to return representative data.
  • U.S. Census TIGERweb — provides district boundary maps; no user data is sent.
  • Google Civic Information API — receives your address to return voter information where available.
  • Google Gemini API — used in the Candidates tab to summarize publicly available campaign information. No user address or personal data is sent to Gemini.

Each third-party service is governed by its own privacy policy. CivicLens is not responsible for how these services handle data they receive directly.

5. Children's privacy

CivicLens is intended for general audiences and is designed for use in educational settings, including middle and high school classrooms. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Because CivicLens does not collect or store any personal data at all, it is appropriate for use by minors in supervised educational contexts.

6. Your rights

Because CivicLens does not retain any personal data, there is no data to access, correct, or delete. If you have questions about how a third-party API used by CivicLens handles your data, please refer to that service's privacy policy directly.

7. Changes to this policy

If CivicLens ever changes how it handles personal data, this policy will be updated and the effective date at the top of this page will reflect the change. Continued use of the app after an update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

8. Contact

Questions about this privacy policy? Reach out via the CivicLens GitHub repository or the contact information listed there.
